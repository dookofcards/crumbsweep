CrumbSweep

Close a tab, lose the crumbs. Cookies are deleted automatically when you close a site's last tab — unless you've whitelisted it.

Add to Chrome View source on GitHub

How it works

1

You close the last tab for a site. A grace period starts — 15 seconds by default, yours to tune from 0 to 5 minutes.

2

Reopen the site in time? Nothing happens. Your session, logins, and carts survive.

3

Otherwise the site's cookies are swept — every subdomain, every cookie store. Whitelisted sites are never touched.

Switching from Cookie AutoDelete?

Cookie AutoDelete was discontinued when Chrome retired Manifest V2. CrumbSweep is built for Manifest V3 from scratch — and it imports your exported Cookie AutoDelete list in one click. Whitelist and greylist carry over automatically.

Features

  • Whitelist: sites that are never cleaned
  • Greylist: sites kept only until browser restart
  • One-click protect-this-site from the popup
  • Whole-site matching (mail.google.com = google.com)
  • Import Cookie AutoDelete JSON or plain domain lists
  • Export your lists as JSON any time
  • Opt-in deep clean: localStorage, IndexedDB, cache
  • Pause auto-cleaning: 15 min, 1 hour, or until you resume
  • Badge counter for cookies cleaned
  • Pinned tabs are never cleaned while pinned
  • Clean any site — or everything but your whitelist — on demand
  • Conservative defaults, no surprises

Zero data collection

No analytics. No telemetry. No accounts. No servers. No network requests of any kind. CrumbSweep's job is deleting data, not gathering it — and because it's open source, you don't have to take our word for it.
